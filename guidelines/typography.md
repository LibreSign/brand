# Typography

The current LibreSign digital typeface is **Montserrat**.

Official web/UI material should use Montserrat in the weights defined by the consuming design system.

## Rules

- prioritize legibility and accessibility;
- keep fallback stacks implementation-specific and documented;
- do not commit third-party font binaries without their upstream license and copyright metadata;
- font files retain their upstream license and do not inherit CC BY-SA from brand artwork or documentation.
