# Naming

Use **LibreSign** with an uppercase L and S and no space between the words.

## Correct
- LibreSign

## Avoid
- Libresign
- Libre Sign
- libreSign
- libresign when referring to the official brand in prose

The repository, website, documentation, presentations, press material, and UI copy should use the official spelling consistently.
