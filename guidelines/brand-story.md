# Brand story

## Libre means freedom

For LibreSign, **Libre** means freedom to operate signing infrastructure, keep control of documents and data, inspect and adapt the software, integrate with existing systems, and choose how the solution is operated and maintained.

That value can be expressed through concrete product benefits:

- self-hosting and infrastructure choice;
- data control and sovereignty;
- inspectable source code;
- interoperability and integration;
- adaptability to organizational workflows;
- reduced dependency on a single proprietary signing service;
- continuity when providers or operational models change.

The brand should not equate “Libre” with “free of charge”. The central idea is freedom and control.
